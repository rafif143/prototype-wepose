# Product Overview

WePose is a visa application platform targeting Indonesian users who need to apply for visas to 35+ countries. The platform simplifies the visa application process by providing professional assistance, transparency, and efficiency.

## Core Features

- Visa search and discovery for 35+ countries
- Interactive visa recommendation quiz
- Visa comparison tool
- Sponsor letter generator with PDF export
- Detailed visa information pages (requirements, pricing, FAQs)
- Blog content for visa-related information
- Multi-language support (Indonesian primary)

## Target Audience

Indonesian travelers and professionals seeking visa services for international travel, with emphasis on popular destinations like Schengen countries, Japan, Korea, Australia, and the United States.

## Brand Identity

- Primary color: Orange (#f97316)
- Secondary color: Navy (#0f1f3d)
- Tone: Professional yet approachable, using casual Indonesian language
- Typography: Poppins (headings), DM Sans (body text)
